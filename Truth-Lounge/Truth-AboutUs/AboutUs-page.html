AboutUs

Truth Lounge and Nightclub was founded by visionary entrepreneur S. Scott Alcius. Alcius created this space where people come together to connect, celebrate, and create lasting memories. The wealth of experiencing in building successful social spaces, Alcius has poured his passion into creating a unique atmosphere that caters to a diverse crowd. At Truth, every detail is intentional—from the immersive lighting and modern design to the carefully curated music that blends the best of hip-hop, Afrobeats, Caribbean rhythms, and international sounds. The goal is simple: create a space where culture is celebrated, energy is contagious, and every guest feels like they belong. More than just a place to party, Truth Lounge and Club is a platform for expression. It’s where emerging talent meets seasoned DJs, where special events turn into unforgettable experiences, and where the community comes alive night after night. Whether you’re celebrating a milestone, networking with like-minded individuals, or just looking for a place to unwind and elevate your night, Truth delivers an atmosphere that is both vibrant and welcoming.
