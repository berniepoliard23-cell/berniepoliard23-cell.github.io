WHO WE ARE

Welcome to Eden SDA Church

Welcome to Eden SDA Church of Miami! We are a community of believers united by our love for God and our desire to grow in faith.

Our church is a place where individuals and families can worship, connect, learn, and experience the love of Christ. We believe that everyone has a place in God's family, and we welcome you to be part of ours.

Whether you are visiting for the first time, looking for a church home, or continuing your spiritual journey, we invite you to grow with us.

What Guides Our Church

Our purpose is centered on faith, service, and sharing the love of Jesus with everyone we encounter.

Our Mission
